Stay safe

Spot refund scams

Real settlements do not require you to pay a stranger to release your money.

Red flags

✕Someone says you must pay a fee, gift card, or crypto to get your refund.
✕A message guarantees a payout or pressures you to act immediately.
✕A page asks for unnecessary sensitive information before explaining the case.
✕A link looks official but does not match the domain shown on the government or court notice.
The FTC says it never requires upfront fees to receive an FTC refund. Check the official list at ftc.gov/enforcement/refunds.

How to verify a settlement

  1. Find the case name and court information.
  2. Confirm the administrator from the court notice or official government source.
  3. Read the full eligibility rules and deadline.
  4. Use the official claim form, and keep a copy of your confirmation.
ClaimFinder is a free helper tool, not affiliated with any court, government agency, or settlement administrator. Search results can be incomplete or outdated; always verify the official case page.
